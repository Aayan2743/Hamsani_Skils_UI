Shipping Methods

At 9Nutz, we ensure your favorite Millets, Healthy Nuts, Sweets, and Namkeens are delivered safely and on time across India. Here’s everything you need to know about our shipping process:
1.
📦 Where We Ship
  • Domestic Shipping: Available across most pin codes in India.
  • International Shipping: We currently do not offer international shipping. (If this changes we'll update the page.)
2.
⏱️ Delivery Timelines
  • Orders are shipped within 1–2 business days after confirmation.
  • Delivery typically takes 3–7 business days post-dispatch for standard orders.
  • Note: We do not process or ship orders on Sundays or public holidays — timelines may vary during festive seasons or high-demand periods.
3.
💰 Shipping Charges

  • Additional charges may apply for bulky items and international orders (if/when available).
  • All fees are displayed clearly before payment at checkout.
4.
🚨 Damaged or Leaked Items

If your order arrives damaged or leaking:

  1. Take clear photos of the damaged item and its packaging.
  2. Email them to help@9nutz.example within 4 days of delivery.
  3. Our team will arrange a replacement or refund promptly after evaluation.
5.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I track my order?
A tracking link will be sent via email/SMS once the order ships. You can track the shipment on the courier partner’s website.
Can I edit or cancel my order?
A: Generally no — we pack and dispatch quickly. Contact support immediately and we’ll try to help if the order hasn’t shipped.
My delivery is delayed — what should I do?
A: Sometimes delays happen due to unforeseen factors (weather, courier issues, festive rush). If your order hasn’t arrived after the estimated window, contact our support and we’ll track it for you.
6.
✉️ Need Help?

For any other questions, please contact our customer support team:

help@9nutz.example

7.
Shipping Options & Delivery Areas
  • Standard Delivery: 3–7 business days (for regular orders).
  • Express Delivery: 1–3 business days (available in select locations).
  • Bulk & Event Orders: Custom delivery schedules are arranged based on event dates and volume.

Delivery Areas:

  • We currently ship across India, covering metros, tier-2, and tier-3 cities.
  • For remote areas or custom delivery requests, our team will reach out after order confirmation.
  • International shipping is currently not available.

Bulk Orders & Event Deliveries

  • Orders must be placed at least 7 days in advance for large orders (weddings, functions, office events).
  • Custom packaging and scheduled deliveries can be arranged.
  • Shipping charges may vary depending on quantity and distance.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, a tracking ID and courier details will be shared via email/SMS. You can track the status of your shipment on the courier’s website.

Delivery Delays

While we strive to ensure timely delivery, delays may occur due to weather conditions, courier issues, festive rushes, or remote delivery zones. We will keep you informed in case of any delay.

Delivery Hours

Deliveries are typically made between 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Sunday or holiday delivery depends on local courier policies.
